Privacy Policy

Prepzy, by Globus Learn · Effective 6 October 2026

This policy explains how Prepzy ("Prepzy", "we", "us"), a product of Globus Learn, handles information in its lecture video service: the system that produces CBSE lecture videos and publishes them to Prepzy's YouTube channel(s). It covers the information we access through YouTube API Services and the information used to run the service.

YouTube API Services. Prepzy uses YouTube API Services to upload and manage its own educational videos. By using a Prepzy feature that connects to YouTube, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

1. Information we access through YouTube API Services

When an authorised Prepzy team member connects a YouTube channel to Prepzy with Google sign-in (OAuth), we access only what is needed to publish and manage Prepzy's lecture videos on that channel:

  • Channel information: the channel's ID and name, to confirm the right channel is connected.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens: to upload videos on the channel owner's behalf.
  • Video information for videos Prepzy uploads: video IDs, titles, descriptions, thumbnails, playlist membership, privacy status and processing/upload status.

We do not access or collect information about people who watch, like, comment on or subscribe to the videos, and we do not read other channels' data.

2. How we use that information

  • To upload lecture videos to the connected channel and set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlists.
  • To check that an upload finished and to record the video's YouTube ID against the lecture it belongs to.
  • To update or remove videos that Prepzy uploaded, when our team asks for it.

We do not use YouTube data for advertising, profiling or any purpose other than publishing Prepzy's own videos, and we do not sell it.

3. Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens and video records are stored on Prepzy's servers (Amazon Web Services, Mumbai region), reachable only by Prepzy systems and authorised staff. We do not share YouTube API data with third parties, except where the law requires it.

To make the videos themselves, the service uses these processors. None of them receives YouTube API data:

ServiceWhat it receives
OpenAINCERT textbook content, to write slides and narration
Google Cloud Text-to-SpeechNarration text, to produce the voice-over
Microsoft OneDrive / SharePointFinished video files, for storage
Amazon Web ServicesHosting of the production system

The videos are made from textbook content. Producing them does not involve students' personal information.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

The video service does not place cookies, tracking pixels or similar technologies on viewers' browsers or devices, and it does not store or read information on the devices of people who watch Prepzy's videos. YouTube may use cookies under its own policies when videos are watched on YouTube.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Video records are kept while the video is published, so we can manage it.
  • OAuth tokens are kept until the channel is disconnected or access is revoked, and are then deleted.
  • If access is revoked, or the channel owner asks us to delete stored YouTube API data, we delete it within 7 days, except any information we are required by law to keep.

6. Revoking access

A channel owner can remove Prepzy's access at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. You can also email us to disconnect the channel and delete the stored data.

7. Security

Access to the production system needs a password or key held by authorised staff. Credentials and API tokens are kept out of source code and stored only on the servers that need them.

8. Children

Prepzy's videos teach school students, but the video service does not collect personal information from children or any other viewers. If you believe a child's personal information has reached us, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to the address below. We will respond within a reasonable time and as required by applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will be shown on this page before they take effect.
