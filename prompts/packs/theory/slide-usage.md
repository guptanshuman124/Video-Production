# WHICH SLIDE TYPE FOR WHICH THEORY CONTENT

- **intro** — every lecture, first slide only: a title card (chapter name, "Lecture N: title") filled in by code. Nothing to write; the narration is a ~10-second welcome.
- **hook** — lecture 1 only, second slide: a scenario, question or image that frames why the chapter matters — no term or date yet.
- **concept_intro** — the plain-language lead-in right before a new term, a timeline or a process is named: the situation or question that makes it needed. An image only when the scenario is visual (a village like Palampur, a "before" map).
- **definition** — a named concept, institution or literary device (Source, Ecosystem, Constitution, Federalism, Imagery): meaning in one line, a few properties or types, optional small table. An image only for categories best seen through examples.
- **timeline** — a span with 3+ meaningful dates, in order (the national movement, the making of the Constitution).
- **cause_effect** — why something happened (3–5 links, each leading to the next) or how a process works (how a bill becomes law, how a water table drops).
- **labeled_diagram** — a map, structure chart, ecosystem diagram or photo that carries the content, read part by part.
- **person** — a key figure or literary character worth a standalone beat: who, role, why they matter here.
- **comparison** — genuinely tabular content: statistics, or two/three things compared across 3–6 bases (two treaties, two characters, federal vs unitary).
- **source_extract** — a primary-source line (speech, Constitution, document) or a short literary extract, with its significance. Word for word from the text only.
- **try_this** — a discussion or activity prompt before a concept is named (study this map, think about your own town) — no answer shown.
- **practice_problem** — 1–3 exam-style questions (2/3/5 marks) for the student to answer, answers withheld; a checkpoint after 1–2 taught ideas.
- **misconception** — only where a genuine, common student error exists: wrong belief vs correct understanding.
- **mcq** — a CBSE-style MCQ with one trap option (a close date, a similar name, a related concept).
- **assertion_reason** — a genuine claim-and-cause pair from this content (Classes 9–12).
- **quick_revision** — end of the lecture: key points + glossary of the terms taught.
