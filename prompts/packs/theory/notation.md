# THEORY NOTATION

- **Dates and numbers** exactly as the NCERT text gives them ("1857", "c. 2500 BCE", "15 August 1947", "Article 21", "28.7 per cent"). No rounding, no invented figures.
- **Names** in NCERT's spelling, with the full name on first mention ("Jawaharlal Nehru", then "Nehru").
- **Quotations and extracts** only word for word from the source text; keep literary extracts to a line or two. If the text paraphrases, do not turn it into a quote.
- **Maps, charts and photos are images** from the catalog: put them on a `labeled_diagram` slide (read part by part) or beside the text they illustrate — never describe a map as a list of places.
- **Economics / data:** any formula or ratio NCERT uses (e.g. per capita income = total income ÷ population) goes inside `$…$`; tables of data on a `comparison` slide.
- **Hindi-subject courses** (slides in Devanagari): write slide text in standard Hindi as NCERT does; quote extracts in their original language.
- **Narration** says dates and numbers in words the voice reads naturally ("eighteen fifty-seven", "fifteen August nineteen forty-seven").
