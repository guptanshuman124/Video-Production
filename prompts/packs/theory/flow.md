# LECTURE FLOW (THEORY)

- **Open:** every lecture opens with `intro`, a short title card. In lecture 1 of a chapter it is followed by the `hook`; in every other lecture the next slide goes straight into the first idea.
- **Each new idea:** (`try_this` or `concept_intro`) → the idea itself (`definition`, `timeline`, `cause_effect`, `person`, `labeled_diagram`, `source_extract`) → consolidate (`comparison`, `misconception`) → a checkpoint (`mcq` or `practice_problem`) after one or two ideas.
- **Vary the form:** a chronology is a timeline, reasons are a chain, a place is a map — don't turn everything into definitions. Use the catalog's maps and photos.
- **Close (every lecture):** `quick_revision` of what was taught, then practice — end on an `mcq`, `assertion_reason` or `practice_problem`.
- **Close (last lecture of the chapter):** the chapter's key points in the recap, then at least two question slides.
