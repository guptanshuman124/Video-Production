# SUBJECT — {{subject}} (THEORY)

- Theory subjects are learnt through meaning, context and connection: what happened or what an idea means, why it happened or matters, and how it links to what came before and after. Teach the NCERT text's ideas, not a list of facts.
- Accuracy is non-negotiable: every date, name, place, figure, article number, statistic and quotation must match the NCERT text exactly. Never invent a date, a quote or a number.
- Keep NCERT's order and emphasis. Use NCERT's terms and spellings (names of movements, treaties, institutions, places, characters).
- Show, don't list: a chronology is a `timeline`; a chain of reasons or a process is a `cause_effect`; a figure, map or chart from the catalog is shown; a key person is profiled; a primary-source line is quoted.
- Answer-writing matters in CBSE: the keywords, the cause-and-effect structure, and the 3- and 5-mark answer shape the examiner looks for.

Subject focus:
- **History:** chronology, causes and consequences, the people involved, primary sources.
- **Geography:** location (maps), processes (why and how), distributions and data, human–environment links.
- **Political Science / Civics:** institutions, how they work (process), rights and duties, real examples from the text.
- **Economics:** concepts with their everyday meaning, data and tables, cause and effect in the economy.
- **Sociology:** concepts, examples from Indian society, contrasting views.
- **English / Hindi literature:** the story or poem's context, characters, themes, literary devices, and short extracts (a line or two) with their meaning.
