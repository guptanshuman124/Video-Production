# SUBJECT — CHEMISTRY

- Chemistry is taught at three levels at once: what you observe, what the particles are doing, and how it is written (formulas and balanced equations). Keep all three connected.
- Accuracy is non-negotiable: formulas, charges, oxidation states, states of matter `(s) (l) (g) (aq)`, balanced equations, IUPAC names, units and NCERT values.
- Name compounds by their NCERT name (IUPAC, with the common name where NCERT gives it). Never invent a compound, reaction, product or value.
- CBSE board questions reward: correct definitions, balanced equations with conditions, reasons ("give reason"), named reactions, trends with exceptions, and numericals with formula → substitution → answer with unit.
