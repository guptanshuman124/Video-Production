# PHYSICAL CHEMISTRY

- Numerical-heavy: every formula taught is used in a `solved_example` or numerical MCQ, with units carried through every step.
- Keep NCERT forms and constants: `R = 8.314 J mol^{-1} K^{-1}`, `F = 96500 C mol^{-1}`, `0.059 V` at 298 K, `2.303` in log forms.
- Graphs (rate vs concentration, conductivity vs dilution, energy profiles) are images read point by point on `image_points`.
- Board favourites: numericals on molarity/molality, rate constant & half-life, Nernst equation, ΔG–E relation; definitions with units.
