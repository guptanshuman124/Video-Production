# ORGANIC CHEMISTRY

- Name every compound by IUPAC (with the common name NCERT uses). Show functional groups explicitly in formulas: `$\ce{CH3CH2OH}$`, `$\ce{CH3COOH}$`.
- Reactions: reactant → product with reagent and conditions over the arrow; name the reaction when NCERT names it (Wurtz, Kolbe, Cannizzaro, aldol, Hoffmann bromamide…).
- Mechanisms (SN1/SN2, electrophilic addition, nucleophilic addition) are taught stage by stage on a `mechanism` slide; the curly-arrow drawing must come from a catalog image — never invent one.
- Board favourites: conversions (A → B in 2 steps), distinguishing tests, reason questions on acidity/basicity/reactivity order.
