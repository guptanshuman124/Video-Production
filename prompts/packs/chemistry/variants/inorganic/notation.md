# INORGANIC CHEMISTRY

- Teach trends across a group and period with their reason, and always name the NCERT exceptions (e.g. anomalous behaviour of the first member, inert-pair effect).
- Electronic configurations written as NCERT does: `[Ar] 3d^{5} 4s^{1}`; oxidation states with signs.
- Coordination compounds: IUPAC names, formula with brackets `$\ce{[Co(NH3)6]Cl3}$`, isomerism, VBT/CFT as NCERT presents them.
- Board favourites: trends with reasons, balanced preparation/property equations, "account for" questions.
