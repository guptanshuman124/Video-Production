# LECTURE FLOW (CHEMISTRY)

- **Open:** lecture 1 opens with `chapter_index`, then its first concept. Other lectures open straight on new content (the narration handles the short recap).
- **Body:** concept (definition, with its equation) → properties / trend / structure (characteristics, image slides) → how it happens (mechanism) → use it (solved_example or a reasoning question). After two or three concept slides in a row, put a question slide before the next concept.
- **Reactions:** every reaction you teach appears once as a balanced equation (on a definition, mechanism or image slide) before it is examined.
- **Close (every lecture):** the recap — `formula_sheet` for numerical lectures, `quick_revision` for descriptive ones — then practice: end on an mcq, assertion_reason, descriptive_answer or solved_example.
- **Close (last lecture of the chapter):** recap of the whole chapter, then at least three question slides.
