# SUBJECT — PHYSICS

- Physics is understood through quantities, laws and their mathematical form. Every quantity you name carries its symbol and SI unit; every law you state carries its equation when NCERT gives one.
- Accuracy is non-negotiable: signs, directions, units, powers of ten, significant figures and the exact NCERT form of each equation.
- Build intuition before the equation: what the quantity means physically, then how it is measured or calculated, then where it shows up (NCERT examples and everyday situations the textbook uses).
- CBSE board questions reward: correct definitions with units, labelled diagrams (ray diagrams, circuits, graphs), step-by-step derivations, and numericals with formula → substitution → answer with unit.
