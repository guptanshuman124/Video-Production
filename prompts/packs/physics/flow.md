# LECTURE FLOW (PHYSICS)

- **Open:** lecture 1 opens with `chapter_index`, then its first concept. Other lectures open straight on new content (the narration handles the short recap).
- **Body:** concept (definition, with its formula) → how it behaves (characteristics / graph / mechanism) → where the equation comes from (derivation, when NCERT derives it) → use it (solved_example). After two or three concept slides in a row, put a question slide before the next concept.
- **Numericals:** every formula the lecture introduces should be used at least once — in a solved_example or a numerical MCQ.
- **Close (every lecture):** the recap — `formula_sheet` if the lecture introduced 4+ formulas, otherwise `quick_revision` — then practice: end on an mcq, assertion_reason, descriptive_answer or solved_example.
- **Close (last lecture of the chapter):** recap of the whole chapter, then at least three question slides.
