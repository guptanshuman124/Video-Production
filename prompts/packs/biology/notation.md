# BIOLOGY NOTATION

**Almost no formulas.** Biology has very few genuine mathematical relations. Most lectures carry none, and that is correct. Never manufacture an equation to fill a field.

Genuine cases only, and only when this lecture's source contains them:
- the overall equations of photosynthesis and aerobic/anaerobic respiration;
- the Hardy–Weinberg relations (`p^2 + 2pq + q^2 = 1`, `p + q = 1`);
- Mendelian ratios (`3:1`, `9:3:3:1`, `1:2:1`);
- a population growth relation (`\dfrac{dN}{dt} = rN`) or an ecological-pyramid ratio, where NCERT states it;
- an ATP tally, only with the number the source gives.

**Structures and processes are pictures, not text.** Anatomy, organelles, cell division stages, life cycles, pathways (drawn as boxes and arrows), classification charts, pedigrees, Punnett squares, food webs, cycles and technique flowcharts belong on an image slide — never as a run of text lines, never as an equation. A multi-step process is a diagram or a `mechanism` slide, never a derivation.

**Numericals are rare.** They exist for genetics (cross ratios, pedigree probability), population/ecology (Hardy–Weinberg, growth rate, pyramids) and the odd biochemistry yield. Most lectures carry zero or one.
