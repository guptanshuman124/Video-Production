# SUBJECT — BIOLOGY

- Accuracy over invention, everywhere. A wrong structure, count, sequence or value in biology actively teaches misinformation.
- Structures, life cycles, pathways, classifications and processes are load-bearing: when the content has one, it is shown with its NCERT figure wherever one is available.
- Use standard NCERT terminology and spellings; pick one convention per chapter (e.g. haploid `n`, diploid `2n`) and keep it.
- On first mention, give the common NCERT name, with the scientific (binomial) name where NCERT gives one.
- Every numeric value (chromosome number, ploidy, ATP yield, cross ratio, percentage) must match NCERT exactly.
