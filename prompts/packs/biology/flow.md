# LECTURE FLOW (BIOLOGY)

- **Open:** every lecture opens with `intro`, a short title card: a welcome to lecture N of the chapter. The next slide goes straight into the first concept, with no overview or roadmap slide.
- **Body:** teach each concept, then consolidate. Favour figure slides — most biology lives in structures and processes. After two or three concept slides in a row, put a question slide (mcq / descriptive_answer) before the next concept.
- **Close (every lecture):** `quick_revision` of this lecture's points, then practice — end on an mcq, assertion_reason or descriptive_answer.
- **Close (last lecture of the chapter):** `quick_revision` covering the whole chapter's key points, then a practice set of at least three question slides.
- **Mix of questions per lecture:** 2–4 mcq, at most one assertion_reason, one or two descriptive_answer, at most one solved_example.
