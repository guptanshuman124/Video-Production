# SUBJECT — {{subject}} (COMMERCE)

- Commerce is learnt by doing: a rule is understood once the student has seen it applied to a transaction, a format filled in, a case decided. Teach the NCERT idea, then show it working on NCERT's own figures.
- Accuracy is non-negotiable: every amount, date, name, rate, ratio, section of an Act and format line must match the NCERT text. Never invent a figure, a provision or a format. Every account you show must balance — the slide checks it in code.
- Keep NCERT's order, terms and formats (Journal, Ledger, Trial Balance, Trading and Profit & Loss Account, Balance Sheet; Fayol's and Taylor's principles in NCERT's wording).
- Show, don't list: a transaction is a `journal_entry`, a posting is a `ledger`, a set of balances is a `trial_balance`, final accounts are `final_accounts` + `balance_sheet`, a rule set is `rule_cards`, a concept or principle is a `principle` (statement → illustration → why it matters).
- Answer-writing matters in CBSE: the correct format, the narration under every entry, working notes, and for theory answers the keywords and the 3- / 4- / 6-mark answer shape the examiner looks for.
