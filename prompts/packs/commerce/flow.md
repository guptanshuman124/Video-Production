# LECTURE FLOW (COMMERCE)

- **Open:** every lecture opens with `intro`. In lecture 1 of a chapter it is followed by the `hook`; other lectures go straight into the first idea.
- **Each new idea:** (`concept_intro` or `try_this`) → the idea (`definition`, `principle`, `rule_cards`) → **apply it** on NCERT's figures (`journal_entry`, `ledger`, `trial_balance`, `final_accounts`, `balance_sheet`, `adjustment`) → consolidate (`comparison`, `misconception`) → a checkpoint (`mcq`, or `practice_problem` + its solution) after one or two ideas.
- **Numerical lectures** follow the book's working order: the transaction → its journal entry → its ledger posting → the trial balance → the final accounts. Show a format filled in before asking the student to fill one.
- **Practice:** a `practice_problem` is always followed by the slide that solves it, with the same figures; never end a lecture on one.
- **Theory lectures** (Business Studies, accounting concepts): principles and rule cards with real examples, comparisons, case studies.
- **Close (every lecture):** `quick_revision` (or `formula_sheet` for a numerical lecture), then end on an `mcq`, `assertion_reason` or `case_study`.
- **Close (last lecture of the chapter):** the chapter's key points in the recap, then at least two question slides.
