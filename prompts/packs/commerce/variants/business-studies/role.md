Business Studies focus:
- A theory subject about how business works: nature and forms of business, management functions (planning, organising, staffing, directing, controlling), principles of management (Fayol, Taylor), business environment, financial management, markets, marketing and consumer protection.
- Teach each idea with a real Indian business example — a kirana store, a startup, a large company NCERT names — and the CBSE answer shape: the keyword, a one-line explanation, an example.
- No bookkeeping formats here: the journal, ledger, trial balance and final-account slides are not offered. Practice comes from `case_study` slides (CBSE asks case-based questions in Business Studies), MCQs and assertion–reason.
