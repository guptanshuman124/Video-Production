Business Studies slide choices:
- A list of principles, functions or features (14 principles of management, functions of marketing) → `rule_cards` a few at a time (each card: the principle, what it says, an example) or a `principle` slide for one that needs room.
- A process in steps (planning process, staffing process, steps of the control process) → `process_flow` or `cause_effect`.
- Two concepts set side by side (Planning vs Controlling, Centralisation vs Decentralisation, Shares vs Debentures) → `comparison`.
- Every lecture's checkpoint is a `case_study` or an `mcq`; the case uses this lecture's ideas so the student can name the concept in it.
