Accountancy focus:
- Class 11: the theory base (concepts, conventions, standards), recording (Journal with golden / modern rules, special journals, Ledger), bank reconciliation, trial balance and rectification of errors, depreciation, provisions, bills of exchange, and the final accounts of a sole trader with adjustments.
- Class 12: partnership (deed, P&L Appropriation, goodwill, admission, retirement, death, dissolution with Revaluation and Realisation Accounts), company accounts (issue and forfeiture of shares, debentures), and the analysis of financial statements (Schedule III, ratios, cash flow).
