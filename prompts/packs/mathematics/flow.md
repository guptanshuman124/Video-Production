# LECTURE FLOW (MATHEMATICS)

- **Open:** every lecture opens with `intro`, a short title card. In lecture 1 of a chapter it is followed by the `hook`; in every other lecture the next slide goes straight into the first idea.
- **Each new idea:** (`try_this` for Classes 6–8) → `concept_intro` → `definition` or `theorem` (+ `proof`) → one or two `solved_example`s → a question (`mcq` or `practice_problem`). Do not state two new ideas in a row without a worked example between them.
- **Worked examples carry the lecture:** every formula or theorem taught is used in at least one `solved_example` or numerical `mcq`.
- **Close (every lecture):** `quick_revision` of what was taught, then practice — end on an `mcq`, `assertion_reason`, `practice_problem` or `solved_example`.
- **Close (last lecture of the chapter):** the chapter's key results in the recap, then at least three question slides.
