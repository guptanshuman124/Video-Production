# LECTURE FLOW (LANGUAGE)

- **Open:** every lecture opens with `intro`. In lecture 1 it is followed by the `hook`, then the `author` (where the textbook introduces one) and, for a story, a short `story_summary`.
- **Literature lectures:** read this lecture's part of the text in order — `passage` slides, each followed when needed by the `word_meanings` of what was just read — then `character`, `literary_device` and `central_idea` where the text supports them, then a check (`mcq` or `practice_problem`).
- **Grammar lectures:** (`concept_intro` or `try_this`) → `grammar_rule` → `grammar_practice` right after each rule → `misconception` for the usual mistakes → `mcq`.
- **Writing lectures:** the purpose → `writing_format` → a model (a `passage` of the sample) → `practice_problem`.
- **Close (every lecture):** `quick_revision` of what was read and learnt, then end on an `mcq`, a `practice_problem` or a `grammar_practice`.
- **Close (last lecture of the chapter):** the text's central idea and key words in the recap, then at least two question slides.
