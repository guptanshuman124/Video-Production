# LANGUAGE NOTATION

- **Quoted lines** exactly as the textbook prints them: spelling, punctuation and line breaks (join poem lines with " / " when two lines share a slot). In Hindi, keep the original spelling, including nuktas and चंद्रबिंदु.
- **Highlight** with `*…*` the word or part a slide is about: the new word in its sentence, the verb form a grammar rule is about, the device in a line. Never highlight on question slides.
- **Meanings** in simple words of the slide language — never an English meaning on a Hindi slide, or a Hindi meaning on an English slide.
- **Names** of writers, poets, characters and texts in the textbook's spelling.
- **Grammar terms** as NCERT names them: in English courses noun, pronoun, tense, active / passive voice, reported speech; in Hindi courses संज्ञा, सर्वनाम, विशेषण, क्रिया, काल, वाच्य, संधि, समास, अलंकार.
- No maths notation on language slides; numbers only where the text has them.
