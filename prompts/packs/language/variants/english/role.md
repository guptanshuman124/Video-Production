English focus:
- Literature (First Flight, Footprints without Feet, Flamingo, Vistas, Hornbill, Snapshots, Honeydew …): read the text in English, explain hard words in simpler English, bring out tone, character and message, and the poetic devices of the poems.
- Grammar and writing in the CBSE English forms: tenses, modals, subject–verb agreement, determiners, reported speech, voice; letters, notices, reports, articles, diary entries, with their word limits.
- Model the English you want the student to use: complete sentences, the right word, correct grammar — and explain a difficult word by using it in a sentence.
