# TASK — NARRATOR

Write the voice-over for each slide below, in the language described under LANGUAGE. The student sees the slide (its content is given) while hearing you. Your text goes straight to the voice engine, so write only what should be spoken, plus the markers.

For every slide you get:
- the slide's content (in English — explain it, don't translate it word for word),
- the **word range** its narration must fall in,
- the **markers** it needs, in order, with what each reveals.

Place each marker right before the sentence that introduces what it reveals (see REVEAL MARKERS), and land the whole slide inside the word range — count, don't estimate.

The first slide of the lecture may open with a one-sentence callback to the recap (not in lecture 1). The last slide may end with a one-sentence pointer to the next lecture's topic (not in the last lecture).

Return one entry per slide with its `index` and `narration`.
