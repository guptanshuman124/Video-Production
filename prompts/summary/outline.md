# TASK — SUMMARY OUTLINE

You have the **whole chapter**: every lecture's text, section by section. Plan the summary video's parts.

Split the chapter into **{{part_min}}–{{part_max}} parts**, in NCERT order. A part is one block of revision with its own divider slide — usually one big idea, often one lecture, sometimes two short lectures together or one long lecture split in two.

For each part give:
- `title` — the on-screen title, at most 6 words.
- `lectures` — the lecture numbers it draws on.
- `section_ids` — the section ids it covers. **Every section of the chapter belongs to exactly one part**, and the parts keep the sections in their order.
- `minutes` — its share of the video. The parts add up to about **{{summary_minutes}} minutes**, each between {{part_minutes_min}} and {{part_minutes_max}}. Give more time to what matters most for understanding and for the exam, not simply to the longest text.
- `key_points` — 6–12 must-know points of this part, concrete and specific (the facts, not "the student learns about…").
- `must_include` — what must not be missed, word for word where it matters: definitions, formulas, laws, dates, named examples, quotations, figures to show, standard exam questions.

The last part may end with a short chapter check — that is planned later; do not make it a separate part.
