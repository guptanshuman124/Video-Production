# CBSE PROFILE

- The audience is a CBSE student studying this chapter lecture by lecture, preparing for school exams and the board exam.
- Teach the NCERT text faithfully and in its order; use NCERT terminology and spellings consistently across the chapter.
- Practice follows the CBSE paper: MCQs, assertion–reason, short-answer (2–3 marks), long-answer (5 marks) and NCERT in-text / exercise-style questions. Prefer questions a CBSE paper would actually ask about this content.
- Explanations aim at understanding first, then at how to write it in the exam (keywords examiners look for, labelled diagrams, correct units).
- Each lecture is a separate video in a series. A lecture may briefly recall what the previous lecture covered and may say what comes next — keep both to a sentence or two.
