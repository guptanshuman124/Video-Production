# CONTINUITY

- You work on one lecture at a time. Build only from this lecture's sections.
- **Recap** (absent in lecture 1) says what the previous lecture covered. Use it for consistent terms and a short opening callback — never re-teach it as new slides.
- **Preview** (absent in the last lecture) lists topics held back for the next lecture. Do not teach them here; at most, point forward to them in one sentence at the end.
- Do not invent details about other lectures beyond what the recap and preview say.
